# Radio

Radio communications work by leveraging the power of the [[Electromagnetism|Electromagnetic]] spectrum to transmit information as fast as physically possible through space.

touching both ends of a battery in the precense of a radio makes a sound in the radio

- The range of the spectrum thats used for this purpose is 1 millimeter to 100 kilometers of wavelength or 3 KHz to 300 GHz of frequency (whereas visible light goes 430 to 790 THz (1000GHz) 380 to 700 nm)
- 2.4 GHz has a wavelength of 12 cm and 5.8 is 5cm
- The waves are generated via [[Antennae]], which are open circuits with a variable [[voltage]] making charges oscilate and thus creating travelling waves ---(~V)---
- The waves are recieved by other [[Antennae]], that when the waves reach them, the [[Fields | Electromagnetic fields]] cause the charges inside to move, generating a [[current]]
- The waves usually transmit information via two encoding systems:
    - AM (amplitude modulation)
    - FM (frequency modulation)
- Resonance is used to filter the particular freq
- The waves are decoded and turned into an electrical signal thats compatible with the amp and speaker (or the device that is using it) (for some reason this uses a diode)
