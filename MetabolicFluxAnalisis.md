# Metabolic Flux Analisis

Metabolites: M1, M2, ..., Mn
Reactions: R1, R2, ... Rm
reactions would be written as 
R: weighted sum of reactants -> weighted sum of products
as an abuse of notation which will work well in the reaction speed balance we can move the reactants to the right side and make their stochiometric coefficients negative

Stochiometric coefficients: Sij (stochiometric coef of Mj in Ri negative if Mj is reactant)
R1: S11 M1 + S12 M2 + ... +  S1n Mn 
R2: S21 M1 + S22 M2 + ... +  S2n Mn
.
.
.
Rm: Sm1 M1 + Sm2 M2 + ... +  Smn Mn 

the speed of reactions is constrained by mass balance
