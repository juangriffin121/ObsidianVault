Normal light switches have two cables the live (L) and the neutral (N). The live one carries AC to the lamp and the neutral recieves it. The switch connects/disconnects the live to the lamp's live cable.
2 way switches allow 2 switches to control one light, this type of switches have 3 wires, a common terminal and L1 and L2, there are multiple ways of connecting them to achieve this, the simplest one is 
- Switches change which Li is connected to the source 
- Li of one switch is connected with Li of the other
- One common is connected to the source and the other is connected to the lamp.
The circuit is complete only when both switches are in the same L, if they are in different ones the circuit isnt closed.
In any configuration any switch can turn on or off the lamp, since whatever Li is in the other switch, and whatever condition (on/off) the lamp is in, changing the state of the switch is gonna change the L, which would change the state of the lamp.
