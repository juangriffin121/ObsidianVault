# Inductors
An electrical circuit component consisiting of a coiled wire, when part of a circuit, this component acts as a force against changes in current.
When current starts flowing in a circuit, it induces current on the coil which creates a magnetic field inside it and a electro motive force against the current.
When current stops flowing in the circuit, the magnetic field changes because of it, and the change in magnetic field induces a current in the wire. 
The effect of the inductor is to smooth out discontinuous changes in current. 
$$V_{ind} = L \frac{dI}{dt}$$
