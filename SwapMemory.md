Since a program's [[Virtual Memory]] is bigger than the real amount of [[RAM]] assigned to it, when the allocation of memory in a program surpases the size of its dedicated space, the [[kernel]] assigns some of the data of the program to the [[Disk Storage]] and so, some of the virtual [[Memory Address]] actually map to the disk.
