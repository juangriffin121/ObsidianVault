- wash the unbounded phages (phages not containing peptides that interact)

- amplify the recovered phages in e-coli culture
- repeat the selection process enriching the pool
- isolate the phages (by plating on bacteria to form plaques or by dilutions (1 bacteria colony one phage genotype)) and sequence the inserts to identify the binding peptides.
